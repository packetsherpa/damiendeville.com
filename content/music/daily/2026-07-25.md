This playlist takes me back to my early 20s in Maryland: Baltimore first, then Laurel, and eventually Bethesda. These were the songs for driving between places, usually with more time than money and no real need to arrive quickly.

<!--more-->

## Why these tracks

The center of it is melodic alternative rock: Drivin N Cryin, The Connells, and The JudyBats, then Sugar, Matthew Sweet, and Bob Mould. Uncle Tupelo and The Jayhawks pull it toward country and roots music without softening its edge. Pete Yorn and Kathleen Edwards carry that same feeling into the early 2000s.

It is guitar-driven, smart, and emotionally plainspoken. Reflective without being sad. A long-drive playlist, windows down, with enough momentum to make the road feel like part of the point.
